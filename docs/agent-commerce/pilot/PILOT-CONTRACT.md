**1. Parties.** This Pilot Service Agreement is between **Qzenta (Pty) Ltd** ("Provider",
[reg no], [address]) and **[Customer name]** ("Customer", [company reg], [address]).

**2. Service.** Provider will monitor the Customer's website **[domain]** ("the Site") for a
30-day pilot period, commencing [date], comprising:
- a baseline Website Health Passport (technical/security and content-accuracy assessment);
- weekly automated scans (content-accuracy against human-approved regulatory ground truth, and
  infrastructure/security checks);
- detection of material changes between scans, with an evidence record;
- alerts to the Customer on material/critical changes;
- a monthly evidence report.

**3. Exclusions.** The Service **does not include** remediation or fixing of the Site, nor any
legal, tax, or compliance advice, nor any guarantee of compliance or of website security.
Remediation, if requested, is a separate engagement. The Customer remains responsible for all
decisions and actions on the Site.

**4. Fees and payment.** Pilot fee: **R499.00** (plus VAT if applicable) per month per Site,
invoiced monthly, payable by EFT within 7 days. The pilot renews monthly unless either party
terminates per clause 8.

**5. Data and confidentiality.** The Service collects only publicly available information about
the Site. Each party keeps the other's confidential information confidential. The Customer owns
the Site and its content; Provider owns the SiteHealth Passport tooling and reports' format.

**6. Limitation of liability.** The Service is provided on an "as is" basis. Provider's total
liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid for the current month. Provider is
not liable for indirect or consequential loss (including loss of clients, revenue, or
reputation), or for actions the Customer takes or fails to take based on the Service.

**7. No guarantee.** The Service provides monitoring and evidence; it does not guarantee that
all issues will be found, that the Site is secure, or that content is compliant.

**8. Term and termination.** The pilot term is 30 days from commencement, renewable monthly by
either party's continuing acceptance. Either party may terminate on 7 days' written notice;
fees for the then-current month remain payable.

**9. Governing law.** This agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa.
